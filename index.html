FAQ

How do I add this to Notion?

Copy your embed link, go to Notion, type /embed, paste the link, and confirm!

Can I choose a different month?

Yes! Use the arrow buttons before copying your embed link.

Can I change the style later?

Yes! Come back to this builder page, update your settings, and copy a fresh embed link.
